Featured · Master's ThesisDigital TwinSimulationSustainabilityChemical Logistics

Master's Thesis Researcher · 2026

SRH University Hamburg · Hamburg, Germany

Thesis title

“Development of a Digital Twin for Sustainable and Compliant Chemical Supply Chain Operations in the EU under ADR and CO2 Regulations”

Overview

My master's thesis develops a conceptual digital twin framework to improve the sustainability, regulatory compliance and operational performance of chemical supply chains in the European Union.

The research explores how digital twin technology, simulation modelling and data-driven decision-making can support chemical logistics while accounting for the ADR (European Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road) and CO2 emissions considerations. Using AnyLogic and Python, the project models chemical supply chain scenarios and evaluates how transport disruptions, demand fluctuations, warehouse capacity and operational improvements affect key performance indicators.

Key research activities

Digital Twin Development:Designed a conceptual model of key chemical supply chain processes: suppliers, transportation, warehousing and customers.

Simulation Modelling:Built AnyLogic scenarios for baseline operations, transport disruptions, increased demand and capacity improvements.

ADR Compliance:Built dangerous-goods transport compliance considerations into the modelling framework.

CO2 Emissions Analysis:Explored an approach to evaluate transport-related emissions and compare environmental performance across scenarios.

KPI-Based Evaluation:Defined KPIs: delivery time, service level, inventory levels, truck utilisation and CO2 emissions.

Scenario Analysis:Investigated how disruptions and alternative planning decisions affect resilience, efficiency and sustainability.

Python-Based Analysis:Used Python for data processing, analytical calculations and evaluation of simulation results.
